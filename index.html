FAQ

Questions, answered.

How does FitMirror 360 create my avatar?

You enter six body measurements: height, chest, waist, hips, shoulder width and inseam. Our AI uses them to generate a 3D digital body with your proportions, ready to try garments on in seconds.

Do I need to upload a photo of myself?

No. Your avatar is built entirely from measurements. The only image you upload is the garment you want to try on.

What kinds of clothes can I try on?

Tops, shirts and tees, dresses, jackets and outerwear, and trousers or skirts. Upload a clear image of the garment and choose its size.

How accurate is the size recommendation?

Recommendations compare your measurements against the garment's size chart and your preferred fit (slim, regular or relaxed), then show exactly where each size is snug or loose so you can decide with confidence.

I run a fashion brand. How do we add FitMirror 360 to our store?

Add a single script tag or install our app for Shopify, WooCommerce or Magento. A FitMirror 360 button appears on your product pages and shoppers try on without leaving your site. Learn more for brands

Is my data safe?

Yes. Measurements are encrypted, never sold, and you can delete your avatar at any time from your account.
