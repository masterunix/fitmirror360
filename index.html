Why we started

Clothes look different on you than they do on the model.

Most brands shoot on very slim models, or occasionally plus-size ones. If you're somewhere in between, it's hard to picture how anything will actually sit on you. So you guess, it arrives, it doesn't fit, and it goes back. A lot of mid-size shoppers told us they just feel unseen.

FAQ

Good questions.

How do you build my avatar?

You give us your height, chest, waist, hips, shoulder width and inseam. We use those to build a 3D body with your proportions. It's saved as your body profile, so next time you can go straight to trying things on.

Do I have to upload a photo of myself?

No. Your avatar comes entirely from your measurements. The only picture you upload is the garment, and a screenshot from the shop's website works fine.

What can I try on?

Tops, shirts and tees, dresses, jackets and other outerwear, and trousers or skirts. Pick the size you're thinking of buying and we'll show you how it sits.

Is the preview exactly how it'll fit?

It's a really good guide, but it isn't a guarantee. Fabric stretch, cut and how a brand makes each piece can all make a difference. That's why we show you zone by zone where a size is likely to be snug or loose, instead of just giving you one letter.

Can I buy things through FitMirror 360?

When you're happy with the fit, hit Buy now and we'll take you to the brand's own website to finish your order.

How much does it cost?

You can start for free. Premium is ₹199 a month, or ₹1,499 a year, and gets you unlimited previews, the full 360° view, brand-wise size suggestions and a saved wardrobe.

I run a fashion brand. How does it work for us?

We add a "Try with FitMirror 360" button to your product pages and connect it to your size charts. Most brands start with a discounted one-month pilot. More on that here

What happens to my measurements?

We only ask for what we need to build your avatar, we tell you how it's used, and you stay in control of it. You can edit or delete your profile whenever you want.
