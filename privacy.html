Privacy Policy

Your body is personal. This policy explains what FitMirror 360 collects, why, and the control you have over it.

What we collect

  • Body measurements you enter (height, chest, waist, hips, shoulder width, inseam) to generate your avatar.
  • Garment images you upload, used only to render your try-on.
  • Account details such as your email if you create an account.

How we use it

We use your data solely to create your 3D avatar, render try-ons and recommend sizes. We never sell your measurements or images, and we never ask for photos of your body.

Brand partners

When you use FitMirror 360 on a partner store, the brand receives an anonymised size recommendation, not your raw measurements, unless you choose to share them.

Your control

You can edit or delete your avatar and saved looks at any time. To request a copy or full deletion of your data, email privacy@fitmirror360.com.

Terms of Use

By using FitMirror 360 you agree to use the service for personal shopping or, for partners, under your signed agreement. Size recommendations are guidance based on the measurements and size charts provided; final fit can vary with fabric and manufacturing.

Only upload images you have the right to use. We may update these terms and will notify account holders of material changes.
