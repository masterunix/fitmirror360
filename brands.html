What's in it for you

Shoppers who can see the fit buy with more confidence.

Not knowing how something will fit is one of the main things that stops people buying clothes online, and a common reason they send them back. FitMirror 360 gives your customers a much better look at the fit before they order.

More confident shoppers

Instead of guessing from a size chart, customers see your garment on a body like theirs and pick a size they're sure about.

Fewer wrong-size orders

Size suggestions come from your own size charts. We track return outcomes during your pilot, so you can see the difference for yourself.

Fit insights

Find out which styles customers think run small or large, and which sizes they actually need, so you can fix it at the source.

4

Pick a plan
